Our Process

Four steps, no surprises.

A clear, well-paced process means fewer decisions made under pressure and a result you will love for years.

Start with a consultation

  1. Discover

    A walkthrough of your space, your routines and the feeling you want to come home to.

  2. Design

    Layouts, palette, concept boards and renderings, refined together until every choice feels right.

  3. Source

    We order, track and inspect every piece, and keep you updated without the back-and-forth.

  4. Reveal

    Delivery, placement and styling, down to the last cushion. You simply walk in.
