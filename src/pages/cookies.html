Cookie Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Priti Interior LLC uses cookies and similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, on this website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. Local storage is a similar browser feature that lets a site remember information between visits. We use both sparingly, and only to make the site work and to respect your choices.

2. Categories we use

CategoryPurposeConsent
Strictly necessaryKeeps your cart, supports secure checkout and remembers your cookie choice.Always active
PreferencesRemembers optional settings to make repeat visits easier.Only with consent
AnalyticsAggregate statistics about how visitors use the site so we can improve it.Only with consent
MarketingMeasures how well our advertising performs.Only with consent

At present our website uses only strictly necessary storage. We do not currently run analytics or advertising tools. If we add any, they will load only after you have opted in to the relevant category, and we will update this policy.
