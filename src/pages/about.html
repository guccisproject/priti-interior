About the Studio

Designed around you, not a trend.

Priti Interior LLC is a Las Vegas design studio creating residential and commercial interiors tailored to the life and vision of each client.

Our Approach

Considered from the first line to the last detail.

Our work begins with thoughtful spatial planning and takes shape through refined material choices and a cohesive palette. The result pairs an elevated aesthetic with the everyday practicality a space truly needs.

We guide each phase personally, from the first layout and design consultation through sourcing, procurement and final installation, so the finished space is thoughtful, polished and unmistakably yours. Clients come to us for a result that feels effortless. They stay with us because the process does too.

Our work is decorative in scope: furniture, color, textiles, lighting selections, window treatments, art and styling. We work within the architecture you have, drawing the very best from it without moving a single wall.

What Guides Us

Our principles

I

Intention

Nothing is included by default. Every piece earns its place through function, proportion or feeling.

II

Restraint

Luxury is found in edit and quality, not excess. A disciplined palette lets beautiful materials speak.

III

Longevity

We favor pieces built to last and designs that will still feel right long after trends have moved on.

IV

Clarity

Fixed fees, written scopes and approvals before every purchase. You always know where things stand.

V

Discretion

Your home and business are private. Photographs and details are only shared with your written permission.

VI

Care

We keep our client list small so every project receives personal, unhurried attention.

Rooted in Las Vegas

Designed for desert living.

Las Vegas light is bright, the heat is real and homes here are made for entertaining. We specify performance fabrics that resist fading, window treatments that soften the glare, and layouts that let a home flow easily from quiet mornings to full houses.

For commercial clients we understand the pace of this city: spaces need to impress, hold up to heavy use and open on schedule.
