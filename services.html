Questions

Good to know

Anything else? Send us a note or call 702-744-7873.

Are furnishings included in the design fee?

No. Design fees cover our time and expertise. Furniture, lighting, textiles, art and accessories are quoted separately with your approval before anything is ordered, and invoiced at the time of purchase with applicable sales tax, freight and delivery.

What happens after I check out?

You will receive a confirmation by email, and our studio will contact you within one business day to schedule your first appointment and share a short questionnaire about your space.

Do you handle remodels or construction?

We focus on the decorative side of interiors: layouts, furnishings, color, lighting selections, window treatments and styling. We do not provide structural, construction or contractor services. If your project needs that kind of work, it should be completed by a licensed contractor before we begin furnishing.

Which areas do you serve?

Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, North Las Vegas and the surrounding valley. E-Design and virtual consultations are available anywhere in the United States.

How long does a project take?

A single-room design typically takes four to six weeks from consultation to final design. Delivery depends on vendor lead times, often eight to sixteen weeks for custom upholstery.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes. Appointments can be rescheduled with 48 hours’ notice, and unused services can be cancelled for a refund within the window described in our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

Not sure where to start?

Begin with a consultation.

Ninety minutes in your space is often all it takes to see clearly what it needs. If you continue with a collection within 30 days, your consultation fee is credited in full.
