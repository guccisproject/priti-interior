Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

We want every client to feel confident booking with Priti Interior LLC. This policy explains how rescheduling, cancellations and refunds work. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions.

1. Rescheduling

You may reschedule any appointment free of charge with at least 48 hours’ notice by email or phone. Appointments cancelled with less than 48 hours’ notice, or missed without notice, may be treated as delivered. We will always try to accommodate genuine emergencies.

2. Cancelling before work begins

If you cancel within 14 days of purchase and before your first appointment has taken place or any design work has started, you will receive a full refund of the amount paid.

3. Cancelling after work begins

Once design work has begun, you may cancel at any time by written notice. We will:

  • charge for services and deliverables already completed, at the listed service price or at our hourly rate of $165 for partially completed work, whichever is lower; and
  • refund any remaining balance of the amount you paid.

If the value of completed work exceeds the amount paid, including under a 50% design retainer, the difference will be invoiced. Design Materials are released once the account is settled.

4. Service-specific terms

ServiceRefund terms
Design consultations (in-home, virtual, commercial assessment)Full refund if cancelled 48+ hours before the appointment. No refund once the consultation has taken place.
Collections (The Refresh, Signature Room, Residence, Estate, Commercial)Full refund per Section 2. After work begins, prorated per Section 3.
Individual design services (layout, palette, selections, e-design, renderings)Full refund before work begins. Non-refundable once the deliverable has been presented.
Design Hours blocksUnused hours refundable within 30 days of purchase. Hours expire 6 months after purchase.
Delivery & Installation Management (monthly)May be cancelled before the next monthly period begins. The current month is non-refundable once started.
Styling and check-in visitsFull refund if cancelled 48+ hours before the visit.

5. Furnishings and third-party goods

Furniture, lighting, textiles, art and accessories purchased through us follow the policies of each vendor, which we will share with your proposal. Custom, made-to-order and special-order items are typically non-cancellable and non-returnable once ordered. Where a vendor accepts a return, any restocking fee, return freight and non-refundable delivery charges are your responsibility. Items damaged in transit will be handled through a claim with the vendor or carrier, and we will help you through that process.

6. How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank may take a further 5 to 10 business days to post the credit. Payment processing fees are not deducted from full refunds.

7. If we need to cancel

If we are unable to perform a booked service for any reason, we will offer a new date or a full refund of fees paid for the undelivered service, whichever you prefer.

8. Satisfaction

If you are unhappy with any part of our work, please tell us. Every collection includes a revision round, and we will do our best to make it right. Because design is subjective, refunds are not provided solely because of a change of taste after a design has been approved.

9. How to cancel

Email contact@priti-interior.com with your name and order reference, or call 702-744-7873. Cancellations are effective on the date we receive them.
