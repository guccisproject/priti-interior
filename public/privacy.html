Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Priti Interior LLC (“Priti Interior,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and in the course of providing our services, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is intended to satisfy the notice requirements of Nevada Revised Statutes (NRS) 603A.300 to 603A.360.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Contact details: name, email address, telephone number and company name.
  • Project details: project address, city and ZIP code, project type, timeline, budget range, notes, and any photographs, measurements or plans you share with us.
  • Order details: the services you book, amounts paid and your payment option.
  • Correspondence: messages you send through our contact form, by email or by phone.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. Your card details are entered directly on Stripe’s secure checkout page and are never received or stored by us. Stripe shares limited information with us, such as your name, email, billing address, the last four digits of your card and the status of your payment. Stripe’s use of your information is governed by its Privacy Policy.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our server receives technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device information and the pages you request. We use your browser’s local storage to keep your cart and remember your cookie choices. See our Cookie Policy for details.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule consultations.
  • To provide, manage and bill for the services you book.
  • To source, order and coordinate delivery of furnishings you approve.
  • To send service-related messages, such as confirmations, receipts, reminders and project updates.
  • To maintain the security of our website and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • With your consent, to send occasional news about our studio. You can unsubscribe at any time.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only as needed with:

  • Service providers who help us operate, such as our payment processor (Stripe), website hosting, email delivery and business software providers. They may only use it to provide services to us.
  • Vendors and delivery partners, such as furniture manufacturers, workrooms, receiving warehouses and delivery or installation crews. They receive only what they need to fulfil your order, such as a delivery name, address and phone number.
  • Professionals you ask us to coordinate with, such as your contractor, architect or property manager.
  • Legal and safety recipients where required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect our rights, property or safety or those of others.
  • A successor business in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

4. Third-party tracking

We do not currently allow third parties to collect personal information about your online activities over time and across different websites when you use our site. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, they will only run with your consent through our cookie settings, and we will update this policy. Our site loads fonts from Google Fonts and images from Unsplash, which receive standard technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver those files.

Our website does not currently respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals, but it does honor your choices in our cookie settings.

5. Retention

We keep project and billing records for as long as needed to provide services and to meet tax, accounting and legal requirements, generally seven years after a project ends. Inquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted within 24 months. You may ask us to delete information sooner, subject to our legal obligations.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access and trusted service providers, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a breach affecting your information occurs, we will notify you as required by NRS 603A.220.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Review and correct: you may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct or update it.
  • Delete: you may ask us to delete your information, subject to legal record-keeping obligations.
  • Marketing: you may opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.
  • Cookies: you may change your cookie choices at any time using the link in the footer.
  • Nevada sale opt-out: under NRS 603A.345, Nevada consumers may submit a verified request directing us not to sell covered information. We do not sell covered information, but you may still submit a request to contact@priti-interior.com and we will respond within 60 days.

To make a request, email contact@priti-interior.com or call 702-744-7873. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. If you live in a state with additional privacy rights, contact us and we will honor your rights as required by the law that applies to you.

8. Children

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page, and for material changes we will post a notice on our website or notify clients by email.
